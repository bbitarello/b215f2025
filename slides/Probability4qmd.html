Probability Wrap Up

  • Probability theory provides a foundation for rigorous statistics.

  • Application of simple rules of probability facilitates the generation of more complex predictions.

  • We can use Bayes’ Theorem to translate between probabilistic statements

Short summary: Multiplication Principle

  • The probability of AND involves multiplication: P[A & B] = P[A] × P[B | A]

  • If the two are independent : P[B | A] = P[B]

  • Therefore, for independent events: P[A & B] = P[A] × P[B]

“All models are wrong, but some are useful”. – George Box (1976), Journal of the American Statistical Association

Probabilities as predictions from a model

A woman wants to have a baby and wants to know the chance that her baby will be a boy.

  • A model is a simplified description of a (usually complex) mechanism.

  • Let’s try thinking of the probability of this future outcome (having a boy) as a prediction for this type of event based on a model.

  • Let’s create a model!

Creating/defining the model

  • Each ovum has an X chromosome; none have Y chromosomes: \(p[X]=1; p[Y]=0\)
  • Half the sperm have one X (no Y) and half have a Y (no X) chromosome: \(p[X]=1/2;p[Y]=1/2\)
  • Only one sperm will fertilize the ovum
  • Every sperm has an equal chance of doing so
  • If winning sperm has X chromosome (\(p=1/2\)), joined with another X from ovum (\(p=1\)) would result in biological sex as determined by XX as female.
  • If winning sperm has Y chromosome (\(p=1/2\)), joined with an X from ovum (\(p=1\)) will result in XY (male).
  • Miscarriages are equally likely for XX or XY fetuses

Creating/defining the model

If you assume this model is true, it’s pretty easy to derive predictions, i.e, probability of XY baby is 50%.

Case Study

Gelman (1998) looked at the populations of countries and the age-adjusted, per capita incidence of kidney cancer (pretty rare cancer, about 15 cases per 100,000 US adults).

  • Results: Countries with lowest per-capita incidence – these tended to be countries with small populations

  • Hypothesis: perhaps something to do with rural environments in those countries?
